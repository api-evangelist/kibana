This Elastic blog post examines how AI-powered security operations centers (SOCs) are transforming cybersecurity defense in the public sector, arguing that traditional security infrastructure cannot keep pace with AI-accelerated threats. Agentic SOCs combine autonomous agents with human oversight to address two critical challenges: system fragmentation and slow response times. Government agencies globally are increasingly adopting these AI-driven platforms to improve threat detection and response while maintaining transparency and human control over security decisions.
